How we work

{{ site_settings.lab_display_name|default:"Aarogyam Pathology" }} collects samples at its centres and at patients' homes, processes routine work at its own head centre, and sends specialised tests to partner laboratories. A qualified pathologist reviews and signs every report.

For tests that go to a partner, we list what each partner charges and leave the choice to you. We are paid a commission by the partner, not by marking up their price - which is why you will not find a "cheapest" badge or a "you save" figure anywhere on this site.

What we will not do

  • {% icon "block" %} We do not interpret your report over the phone. That is your doctor's work.
  • {% icon "block" %} We do not take payment on this website, and we will never ask for a card number or an OTP.
  • {% icon "block" %} We do not sell or share patient information, and we do not publish anyone's results.
  • {% icon "block" %} We do not dispense Schedule X or habit-forming medicines against an online request.
