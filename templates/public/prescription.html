What happens next

  1. 1

    Our registered pharmacist reads the prescription and checks it is valid and complete.

  2. 2

    We call you with what is in stock, what it costs and when you can collect it.

  3. 3

    You confirm on the call. You pay when you collect - never on this website.

{% icon "lock" %} Your prescription stays private

The file goes to our staff only. It is not published anywhere on this site and it is not shared with anyone outside the pharmacy. There is no public address at which anyone could reach it.

{% icon "warning" %} What we cannot dispense

We cannot supply Schedule X drugs or habit-forming medicines against a photo. Those need the original prescription in person. If the prescription is unclear or out of date, we will call you rather than guess.

A good photo

  • {% icon "check" %} All four corners of the paper in frame
  • {% icon "check" %} The doctor's name, registration number and date readable
  • {% icon "check" %} Flat on a table, in daylight, no flash glare
